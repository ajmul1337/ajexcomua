# AJEX landing page

## What I’ll build
- A polished Ukrainian-language homepage for finding and ordering auto parts.
- A compact top bar, clear AJEX header, premium automotive intro, and strong Telegram, Viber, phone, and catalog actions.
- A working selection panel with Auto, VIN, and license-plate modes that prepares a request for Telegram.
- Dense category and popular-product sections with visible prices, availability, and direct inquiry actions.
- Trust sections covering nearly 10 years in the market, suppliers, delivery, VIN matching, the supplied YouTube video, Instagram, and a small location map.
- Search-friendly content for major car brands and parts queries without keyword stuffing.

## Visual direction
- Restrained European parts-store styling using the supplied cold light background, navy, white, and selective gold accents.
- Manrope typography, compact controls, thin borders, soft shadows, small corner radii, and realistic product imagery.
- No racing imagery, carbon textures, oversized car photography, glass effects, excessive animation, or red-led styling.

## Technical details
- Build the page at the main address and define reusable data-driven sections for categories, products, and brands.
- Use semantic site-wide style tokens and responsive layouts for desktop and mobile.
- Wire all external links to the supplied AJEX catalog, Telegram, Viber, phone number, Instagram, YouTube, and address map.
- Add page-specific search and social metadata, then verify the page visually at desktop and mobile sizes.
